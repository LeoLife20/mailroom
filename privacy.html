Mailroom — Privacy policy · Updated 25 October 2026

Privacy
policy.

Mailroom is a desktop application that runs only on its owner’s Mac. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What Mailroom accesses

With your permission, Mailroom uses the Gmail API (scope gmail.modify) to read message metadata and content in your inbox, and to change labels — marking messages read or unread and archiving them. It also reads your account’s email address and name to label the account in the app. Mailroom does not send or delete email.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. All processing happens locally on your Mac. OAuth tokens and a cache of recent message subjects and snippets are encrypted with the macOS Keychain and stored in the app’s local data folder. Message bodies are fetched on demand and are not written to disk. Mailroom has no servers and sends no data to the developer or to any third party.

Sharing and advertising

Google user data is never sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising. Mailroom’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Removing an account in Mailroom’s settings revokes its access token with Google and deletes its local data. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
